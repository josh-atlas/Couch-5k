# Couch-5K 🏃

A personal training app to go from couch to 5K finish line.

## My Race

**LMJS Fourth Sunday Race** — May 24, 2026 · Oakland, CA

## The Program

9-week progressive run/walk training plan. 3 runs per week. Built around the classic Couch to 5K structure.

|Week|Theme             |Total Time|
|----|------------------|----------|
|1   |First Steps       |~20 min   |
|2   |Finding Rhythm    |~22 min   |
|3   |Stretching Further|~25 min   |
|4   |Building Endurance|~28 min   |
|5   |The Leap          |~30 min   |
|6   |No Looking Back   |~30 min   |
|7   |Continuous Runner |~25 min   |
|8   |Almost There      |~28 min   |
|9   |Race Ready        |~30 min   |

## The App

Single-file HTML app (`run5k.html`). Open in any mobile browser and bookmark to home screen.

**Features:**

- Weekly training plan with progressive intervals
- Per-workout completion tracking
- Weeks unlock sequentially — no skipping ahead
- Repeat any week if you need to consolidate
- Run log with dates
- Overall progress tracking

## Usage

No install. No dependencies. No accounts.

1. Open `run5k.html` in your phone browser
1. Bookmark to home screen
1. Log each run after you complete it
1. Show up on May 24
